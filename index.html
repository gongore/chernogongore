Percentage Change Formula

((Final Amount - Initial Amount) / Initial Amount) × 100

Example: $100 increasing to $120 represents a 20% gain.

Loss Recovery Formula

(Percentage Loss / (100 - Percentage Loss)) × 100

Example: A 50% loss requires a 100% gain from the reduced value to break even.
